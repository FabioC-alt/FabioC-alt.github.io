OctoLoRA — gradient-adaptive LoRA fine-tuning

2026
PythonPyTorchPEFT / LoRA+SLURMGSM8K

A custom LoRA variant fine-tuning Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on GSM8K math reasoning. A hand-rolled OctoLoRALayer replaces frozen attention projections and adds a per-layer gating mechanism that controls how much gradient reaches the A matrix during backprop, driven by adaptive learning-rate ratios between the LoRA matrices — paired with a LoRA+ optimizer that assigns separate learning rates to A and B.

  • Distributed training on a SLURM cluster.
  • Ablation harness with toggleable --gate / --lora-plus flags and four configuration variants for isolated testing.
  • Found and documented six pipeline bugs — including a data-handling error that trained the model on constant boilerplate instead of the questions — with an honest write-up of what each invalidated.
73.39% GSM8K accuracy (3 seeds) · +1.3 pts over vanilla LoRA (~72.10%)
